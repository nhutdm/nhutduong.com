2025 was about expansion: new technology at work, a new apartment for the family, and progress on the plans started in 2024. These are the moments that shaped the year.

## Professional Achievements

### Back to Full-Stack Development

2025 marked my return to full-stack development with a modern technology stack. After years of working primarily with SAPUI5:

- Learning SAP CAP for Node.js to build microservice architecture for our Next-Generation Solution
- Returning to Angular development after a long hiatus, with fresh eyes on frontend architecture
- Working through the full-stack workflow with SAP CAP and Angular on SAP BTP Platform

**Result:** I can now architect and implement complete solutions from backend to frontend on our stack.

### Designing the Next-Generation Solution

Building on last year's user experience work, I became deeply involved in designing our next-generation solution from the ground up. It taught me a lot about enterprise project planning:

- Taking part in long technical discussions to architect the solution from scratch
- Leading evaluation phases from cost estimation to pros and cons analysis to pick the approach
- Learning how long-term projects get initiated inside enterprise companies

The work shaped our product roadmap and gave me a view into strategic decision-making at the organizational level.

### New Tools at Work

- Implementing many requirements in Spring Boot to support and enhance our current product
- Using AI tools like Vibe Coding to speed up learning and solve daily challenges
- Using AI for Angular component development, cutting development time while keeping quality

Each new tool made me more productive and made some slow problems faster to solve.

## Personal Growth

### Moving to a New Apartment

My family and I moved to a new apartment in the commune area of Binh Chanh, Ho Chi Minh City, for practical reasons:

- Much lower rent, easing financial pressure
- A leafy community with many young people working toward goals of their own
- Cheaper food, though the tradeoff was a longer commute to work

Moving further from the center moved us forward financially, and community matters as much as location.

### Learning to Drive

In March 2025 I started learning to drive, working toward a C1 truck driver license in Vietnam, mostly to make family life easier:

- Completed the DAT (Distance and Time) section of the training course by October 2025
- Planning to take the examination in 2026 to get the license
- Goal: buy a used car afterward, for more flexibility and less commute stress

### Family

The new environment has been good to us:

- A greener, more peaceful place for the kids to grow and play
- Neighbors who are building their own futures
- Family time stayed a priority despite the longer commutes

The trade between rent money and quality of life worked out, with some planning.

### Property Investment Progress

Our Long An Province investment had an eventful 2025:

- One month after we received the apartment, the province was administratively changed from Long An to Tay Ninh Province, an unexpected turn in the investment story
- Leased the apartment at a 3% ROI, which helps pay down the bank debt
- Received the Certificate of Land Use Rights just 4 months after getting the apartment, a fast process thanks to Nam Long
- Worked through the loan process with BIDV Bank and got a 5.5% per year interest rate

**Result:** the financing lets me pay off the debt within 3 years without financial pressure.

## Lessons Learned

- **Keep learning**: returning to Angular with fresh eyes while learning SAP CAP kept my skills relevant
- **Compare options**: from apartment moves to loan negotiations, comparing options leads to better outcomes
- **AI as an amplifier**: AI tools don't replace expertise, they amplify productivity and speed up learning
- **Money decisions**: where you live and how you structure investments affect daily stress
- **Practical skills**: learning to drive is an investment in the family's future convenience

## Looking Forward to 2026

My goals for 2026:

- Deepening my expertise in SAP BTP, SAP CAP, and Angular toward full-stack architect work
- Contributing to the successful delivery of our next-generation solution
- Trying more AI-powered development tools
- Completing the driver's license examination and moving toward buying a family car
- Possibly upgrading to a more convenient location as our finances improve
- Managing our Tay Ninh property investment
- More family time despite busy work

## Thank You

Thank you to my wife, whose support makes everything else possible, and to my children, who take every change in stride and fill the house with noise. And to my colleagues and mentors: thank you for the shared work and the shared learning.

2025 asked for a lot of pivots, from new technology stacks to a cheaper apartment to driving school. Every decision was made with the long term in mind.
