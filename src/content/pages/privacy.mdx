## What this site does not do

- **No cookies.** Nothing is set, read, or shared.
- **No analytics or tracking.** No page-view counting, no fingerprints, no ad pixels, no A/B scripts.
- **No third-party scripts.** The only JavaScript on this site powers the table of contents and view transitions, and it is served from this domain. Fonts are self-hosted.
- **No accounts, forms, or newsletters.** There is nothing to sign up for; the RSS feed carries content only.

</div>

## What is logged

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes each request — including your IP address and browser headers — to serve pages and protect against abuse. That processing is governed by [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). This site's owner has no access to identifiable visitor data beyond what Cloudflare retains in the ordinary course.
